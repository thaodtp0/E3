💡 Grammar Key Points / Tóm tắt ngữ pháp:

  • 1. Used to + Verb (bare-infinitive): Describes past habits or states that no longer happen now.
    (Diễn tả thói quen/trạng thái trong quá khứ nay không còn nữa. Vd: I used to go to school on foot.)
  • 2. Articles (a / an / the / Ø):
    - a/an: Singular countable nouns mentioned for the first time.
    - the: Specific/known nouns.
    - Ø (No article): Uncountable or plural nouns in general.
  • 3. No article vs. The (School context):
    - go to school / be at school: Primary purpose (studying).
    - go to the school: Visiting the physical building specifically.
