💡 Key Grammar Summary Covered:

  • 1. Passive Voice (Present & Past Simple): Am/Is/Are + V3/ed (e.g., Tuna is sold in many supermarkets) & Was/Were + V3/ed (e.g., The tourists were guided through the cave).
  • 2. Adj + to V structure: (e.g., It was great to watch the dolphins swimming in the ocean.)
  • 3. Emphasis with "even": Used to highlight something surprising (e.g., Even young children know that lions are mammals.).
  • 4. Indefinite Pronouns: somewhere, nowhere, everywhere, anywhere (e.g., Wild animals can be found everywhere in this park.).
