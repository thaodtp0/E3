💡 Key grammar summary covered:

  • 1. Reported Speech: Shifting tenses back (e.g., Present Simple ➔ Past Simple). "I watch news" ➔ He said he watched news.
  • 2. Past Perfect: Had + V3/ed for actions before another past point. (e.g., The show had finished before I turned on the TV.)
  • 3. Should for suggestions & offers: Should I / You should + V (e.g., Should I change the channel? / You should follow breaking news.).
